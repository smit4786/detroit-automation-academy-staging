What you are looking at

Route shapes and stop positions come from the agencies’ published GTFS schedule data; the bus markers are live vehicle positions from the agencies’ own feeds, published about every five minutes. Zoom in close and the markers resolve into true-scale bus models — each DDOT bus is matched to its fleet model (dimensions and seating from the DDOT fleet roster) and oriented to its reported heading. It is the same live layer that powered the retired Forge Line page’s bus count — the Forge Line concept itself was modeled on the transit Detroit already runs.

About this service

Route shapes & stops: DDOT, SMART, and QLINE GTFS. Bus dimensions & seating: DDOT fleet roster via CPTDB wiki. Street underlay: U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line 2025 (Wayne, Oakland & Macomb counties). Live data: DDOT via Clever Devices BusTime; SMART via Swiftly GTFS-Realtime; QLINE via its public GTFS-RT feed. Trademark notice: DDOT is a service mark of the City of Detroit. SMART is a service mark of the Suburban Mobility Authority for Regional Transportation. This is an unofficial project by Detroit Automation Academy — not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of DDOT, SMART, or the City of Detroit.
